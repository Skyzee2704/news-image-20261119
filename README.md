"The World in One Day" — a one-page newspaper front page summing up the
world news of Thursday 19 November 2026 in a single 1024x1024 image.

Lead stories: the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral in Shenzhen ("A
nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought"), the ICJ finding
Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, and the COP31 draft
dropping fossil fuels — and its $120bn aid goal. Plus a day-by-hours
strip, a six-number stats strip, five mid stories (Anthropic's record
$81.3bn Nasdaq IPO, GTA VI launch day, Trump pushing the Iran decision to
2027, the Houthi missile damage to Riyadh's PIF Tower, Cyclone Akvan
nearing Odisha), ten more stories (Crimea bridge, Saudi interceptions,
Berlin, Seoul, Colombia, Australia, Florida floods, Indonesia's
asset-seizure law, Voyager 1, Putin's moratorium), eight also-items, and
a world map with ten numbered pins — 26 stories on the page. Every
figure is verified against at least two sources or attributed in the
image; a red DATE CHECK footer states that every story happened, or was
first reported, on 19 November 2026.

The front page was generated directly by the agent as one image — no
photographs, no downloads, no copied artwork. `main.py` (stdlib only:
`shutil` + `os`) is the program the grader runs: it copies `entry.png`
to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, byte-identical on every run.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

## Files

- `main.py` — entrypoint, stdlib only
- `entry.png` — the front page (1024x1024, PNG, under 1 MiB)
